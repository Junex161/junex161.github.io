---
title: Plum Crumble Traybake
description: A whole baking tray of shortcrust, fresh plums and buttery cinnamon crumble.
category: baking
order: 23
servings: "1 baking tray"
time: 1 h 50 min
ingredients:
  - "Base:"
  - 400 g plain flour
  - 140 g sugar
  - 2 sachets vanilla sugar
  - 2 pinches of salt
  - 240 g vegan margarine, plus some for the tray
  - "Crumble:"
  - 270 g plain flour
  - 150 g sugar
  - 1½ pinches of salt
  - 1 tsp cinnamon
  - 180 g vegan butter (e.g. Alsan)
  - "Topping:"
  - 1.25–1.5 kg plums
  - 6 tbsp breadcrumbs
---

1. For the shortcrust, mix the flour, sugar, vanilla sugar and salt. Add the margarine and quickly knead into a smooth dough. Wrap it and chill for at least 30 minutes.
2. Wash and quarter the plums.
3. For the crumble, mix the flour, sugar, salt and cinnamon in a large bowl, add the vegan butter and knead into a rough dough. Rub it between your hands into crumbles and set aside.
4. Line a baking tray with a baking mat or paper and grease the edges with margarine.
5. Roll out the dough on the tray and pull it up a little at the edges. Prick it several times with a fork and sprinkle the breadcrumbs over it.
6. Spread the plums on top, then the crumble.
7. Bake on the lowest rack at 180 °C (top and bottom heat) for about 50 minutes, until the crumble is golden.

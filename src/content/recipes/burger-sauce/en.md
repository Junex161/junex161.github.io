---
title: Burger Sauce
description: A quick creamy sauce from ketchup, vegan mayo and crème fraîche. Made for the mushroom burgers.
category: side
order: 41
time: 5 min
ingredients:
  - 6 tbsp ketchup
  - 3 tbsp vegan mayo
  - 1 tbsp vegan crème fraîche
  - 1 tsp mustard
  - ½ tsp sugar (optional)
  - Salt, pepper, paprika
tip: Add anything else you like, such as chopped gherkins, a little smoked paprika or a dash of hot sauce.
---

1. Put all the ingredients in a small bowl.
2. Stir until smooth and season with salt, pepper and paprika to taste.

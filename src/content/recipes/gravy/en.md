---
title: Vegan Gravy
description: A rich brown gravy from mushrooms, red wine and thyme. Perfect with Spätzle.
category: side
order: 40
servings: "2–4 people"
time: 30 min
ingredients:
  - 1 tbsp vegan butter or oil
  - 1 onion, finely diced
  - 150 g mushrooms, finely diced
  - 1 carrot, finely diced
  - 1 clove of garlic, chopped
  - 1 tbsp brown sugar
  - 1 tbsp flour
  - 60 ml red wine
  - 1 tbsp red wine vinegar or balsamic
  - 1 tbsp soy sauce
  - 300 ml vegetable stock
  - 1 tbsp tomato paste
  - 1 tsp mustard
  - 1 sprig of thyme
  - 1 bay leaf
  - ½ tsp pepper, salt to taste
---

1. Heat the butter or oil in a large pan and fry the onion and mushrooms over high heat for about 3 minutes, until the onion is translucent and the mushrooms start to brown.
2. Add the carrot and garlic and fry for another minute, then turn the heat down a little and let everything soften for 3 more minutes.
3. Stir in the sugar and flour. Deglaze with the red wine, vinegar, soy sauce and stock, then stir in the tomato paste and mustard.
4. Add the thyme and bay leaf and simmer uncovered over low to medium heat for 10–15 minutes, until the gravy has thickened.
5. Remove the bay leaf and blend the gravy smooth with a blender or hand blender. Season with salt and pepper to taste.

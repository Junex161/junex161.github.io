---
title: Linzer Lattice Tart
description: Shortcrust with ground hazelnuts and cinnamon, filled with red jam under a lattice top.
category: baking
order: 25
servings: "1 tart (26 cm)"
time: 75 min
ingredients:
  - "Shortcrust:"
  - 300 g flour (wheat, spelt or gluten-free)
  - 125 g ground hazelnuts or almonds
  - 1 tsp baking powder
  - Pinch of salt
  - 1 tsp cinnamon
  - Pinch of gingerbread spice, cardamom or cloves (optional)
  - 120 g cane sugar or other sugar
  - 150 g vegan butter, cold and cut into small cubes
  - 6 tbsp cold water
  - "Filling:"
  - 250 g red jam of your choice or homemade compote
tip: Chill the tin first. A cold tin keeps the shortcrust cold while you press it in.
---

1. Grease a 26 cm tart or springform tin and put it in the freezer for a few minutes.
2. Mix the flour, ground hazelnuts, baking powder, salt, cinnamon, spice and sugar in a bowl. Add the cold vegan butter and the water and knead everything by hand (or in a food processor) into a smooth dough. Divide into 2 portions and shape each into a flat ball.
3. Wrap one portion tightly in cling film and keep it in the fridge for the lattice.
4. Roll out the other portion on cling film or baking paper, lay it in the tin and press it evenly onto the base and up the sides.
5. Spread the jam over the base and put the tart in the fridge so the dough stays firm.
6. Preheat the oven to 180 °C (top and bottom heat).
7. Roll out the second portion on cling film or baking paper and cut it into strips. Lay the strips over the jam with a little space between them to form a lattice and press them gently onto the edge.
8. Bake for 25–30 minutes until golden brown.
9. Let the tart cool in the tin before taking it out.

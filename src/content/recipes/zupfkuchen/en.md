---
title: Zupfkuchen
description: A German classic. Dark chocolate shortcrust with a hint of coffee, a creamy vanilla yogurt filling and pinched-off crumbles on top.
category: baking
order: 22
servings: "1 springform tin, 22 cm (12 pieces)"
time: 2 h 20 min
ingredients:
  - "Base:"
  - 200 g plain flour (type 550)
  - 100 g fine cane sugar
  - 4 tsp vanilla sugar
  - 2 tsp baking powder
  - 2 tbsp cornstarch
  - 4 tbsp cocoa powder
  - 1 tbsp instant coffee, finely ground
  - 1 pinch of salt
  - 125 g vegan margarine
  - "Filling:"
  - 40 g cornstarch
  - 5 tbsp vanilla soy milk
  - 500 g vanilla soy yogurt
  - 110 g fine cane sugar
  - 1 sachet vanilla sugar
  - Juice and zest of 1 organic lemon
  - 125 g vegan margarine
tip: If you like it extra creamy, double the filling and use a 24 or 26 cm tin. It then needs about an hour in the oven.
---

1. Mix the flour, sugar, vanilla sugar, baking powder, cornstarch, cocoa, coffee and salt. Add the margarine in small pieces and knead into a dough, with a few drops of water if it's too firm. Wrap it in cling film and chill for 1 hour.
2. Heat the oven to 160 °C and line the tin with baking paper. Press about three quarters of the dough thinly into the bottom and up the sides to roughly 4 cm. Prick it with a fork and put it back in the fridge.
3. For the filling, whisk the cornstarch with the soy milk until smooth, then stir in the yogurt, sugar, vanilla sugar, lemon juice and zest. Melt the margarine over low heat and stir it in once it's only lukewarm.
4. Spread the filling over the base. Pinch the rest of the dough into small crumbles and scatter them on top.
5. Bake on the middle rack for about 45 minutes, until a skewer comes out clean. Let it cool completely in the tin.

---
title: Banana Pancakes
description: Fluffy spelt pancakes sweetened with ripe banana and vanilla, served with berries and maple syrup.
category: breakfast
order: 30
servings: "2–3 people"
time: 30 min
ingredients:
  - "Pancakes:"
  - 2 ripe bananas
  - 1 vanilla pod (or a little less sugar plus vanilla sugar)
  - 250 g spelt flour (type 630)
  - 1 sachet baking powder
  - 1 pinch of salt
  - 50 g cane sugar
  - 250 ml almond or oat-almond drink
  - Coconut oil or margarine for frying
  - "Topping:"
  - 300 g mixed berries
  - 8 tbsp maple syrup
tip: Keep the finished pancakes warm in the oven at 70 °C (fan) while you fry the rest.
---

1. Mash the bananas with a fork.
2. Slit the vanilla pod lengthwise, scrape out the seeds and mix them into the banana.
3. Sift the flour and baking powder into a bowl. Add the banana, salt, sugar and plant milk and stir into a smooth batter. Let it rest for 10 minutes.
4. Heat a little oil in a non-stick pan and add a small ladle of batter per pancake. Fry over medium heat until small bubbles form on top, then flip and fry until golden on both sides. Add more oil now and then.
5. Wash and drain the berries, then serve the pancakes with berries and maple syrup.

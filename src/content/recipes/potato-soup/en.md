---
title: Potato Soup
description: A simple, creamy potato soup, blended smooth with a splash of coconut milk.
category: soup
order: 10
servings: "2–3 people"
time: 30 min
ingredients:
  - 500 g potatoes (or a bit more)
  - 1 onion
  - 3 cloves of garlic
  - Some celery (optional)
  - About 800 ml vegetable stock
  - A splash of coconut milk
  - A splash of oat milk (optional)
  - Salt, pepper, nutmeg
---

1. Peel or wash the potatoes and cut them into small cubes.
2. Chop the onion and garlic and sweat them in a pot with a splash of the stock until translucent.
3. After a few minutes, add the potatoes and the chopped celery and let them fry briefly.
4. Pour in enough stock to cover the vegetables and simmer for 5–10 minutes, until everything is soft.
5. Blend until creamy with a hand blender, adding coconut milk and oat milk as you go.
6. Season with salt, pepper, nutmeg and any other spices you like.
